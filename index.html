Unit Database

Balance is checked with node tools/balance.js, which runs thousands of equal-cost battles in the real game engine. Every unit has clear counters, and no faction wins more than ~55% of random equal-cost army matchups.

How to Play

Goal

Destroy every enemy structure. Lose all of yours and you are defeated.

Economy

Workers harvest kyber crystals and tibanna gas (build a refinery on a gas vent first). Build supply structures to raise your supply cap.

Tech

HQ → Barracks → Factory → Hangar. The Research structure unlocks each faction's heavy units and bombers. Towers defend vs ground and air.

Counters

Damage bonuses (e.g. +8 vs light) decide fights. Hover any button for strong/weak matchups. Air units can only be hit by anti-air weapons.
