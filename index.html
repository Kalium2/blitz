HOW TO PLAY

OFFENSE

  • Snap the ball with ACTION.
  • Point the stick at a receiver and press ACTION to pass. Stick neutral throws to the most open man (the pulsing ring).
  • Ball carrier: ACTION = spin, SPECIAL = hurdle, hold TURBO to burst.
  • Hit SPECIAL as the ball arrives to jump for the catch.

DEFENSE

  • Before the snap, ACTION cycles which defender you control.
  • Near the ball carrier, ACTION = dive tackle. Otherwise it switches to the defender closest to the ball.
  • SPECIAL = jump to pick off passes.
  • After the whistle, ACTION delivers a late hit. No flags in Blitz!

RULES

  • 7 on 7. 30 yards for a first down.
  • 3 straight completions, or 3 straight defensive stops (sacks, picks, stuffs), puts your team ON FIRE: unlimited turbo and boosted stats until the other team scores.
  • Punt and field goal are on the play-select screen.
